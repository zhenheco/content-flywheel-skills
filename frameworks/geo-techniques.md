# GEO Techniques — write content AI engines cite

> Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): make your content the answer that ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews quote. Adapted from [aaron-he-zhu/seo-geo-claude-skills](https://github.com/aaron-he-zhu/seo-geo-claude-skills) (Apache-2.0).

## 1. Definition optimization

Lead with the term, give its category and primary function, keep it 25–50 words, and make it stand alone as a complete answer.

**Template:** `"[Term] is [category] that [primary function], [key characteristic]."`

- ❌ "SEO is really important for businesses and involves various techniques…"
- ✅ "**Search Engine Optimization (SEO)** is the practice of optimizing websites and content to rank higher in search results, increasing organic traffic and visibility."

Checklist: starts with the term · clear category · primary function · precise language · stands alone · 25–50 words.

## 2. Quotable statements

Generative engines lift self-contained, attributable sentences. Write them on purpose:

- **Statistics** — specific number + source + context (timeframe, comparison).
- **Facts** — verifiable, unambiguous, authoritative source.
- **Definitions** — see above.
- **Comparisons** — "Unlike [A], [B] [specific difference], which means [implication]."
- **How-to steps** — numbered, action-first.

## 3. Authority signals

- Author byline with real credentials
- Expert quotes with attribution
- Citations to peer-reviewed or primary research
- References to recognized authorities
- Original data or research
- Named case studies
- Industry statistics with sources

## 4. Structure for generative engines

- **Q&A sections** — `## What is [Topic]?` followed by a 40–60 word direct answer.
- **Comparison tables** — feature rows + a "Best for" row.
- **Numbered lists** for process/how-to queries.
- **Definition boxes** — `> **Key Definition:** [Term] refers to [clear definition].`

## 5. Factual density

Prefer specific, verifiable, attributable facts over vague generalities. One concrete, sourced sentence beats a paragraph of hedging — and is far more likely to be cited.
